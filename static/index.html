Yat is a decentralized financial platform built on DAG and Ed25519 cryptography, offering a scalable, secure, and transparent alternative to traditional finance. With no blocks or mining, it enables parallel transactions and true financial freedom. Powered by iFEHU for governance, Yat is shaping the future of Web3 finance.
